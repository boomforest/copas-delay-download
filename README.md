# Copas Delay — for Logic and Ableton (Mac)

**Download:** [CopasDelay-Mac.zip](https://github.com/boomforest/copas-delay-download/raw/main/CopasDelay-Mac.zip)

1. Unzip it.
2. Double-click **INSTALL** (if your Mac blocks it: right-click → Open).
3. Restart your DAW.
   - Logic: Audio Units → Casa de Copas → Copas Delay
   - Ableton: Plug-ins → VST3 → Casa de Copas → Copas Delay (if missing, turn on VST3 in Preferences → Plug-ins and Rescan)

Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. Made by Casa de Copas.
